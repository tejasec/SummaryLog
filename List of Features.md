**SummaryLog** - **notifications Summarizer**  


SummaryLog helps you save, organize, search, and manage notifications received on your Android device. Important notifications can disappear in seconds after being dismissed, cleared, or replaced by newer alerts. SummaryLog automatically captures and stores notifications locally on your device so you can review them whenever you need and summarize it.  
  
SummaryLog keeps a searchable history of notifications that have appeared on your phone.  
  
**WHY USE SUMMARYLOG?**  
  
Notifications often contain important information that becomes difficult to find once dismissed. SummaryLog creates a secure notification archive directly on your device, helping you keep track of alerts, messages, reminders, and updates without relying on cloud storage or external servers and a totally offline model for summarizing it.  
  
The app works quietly in the background and automatically records supported notifications as they arrive. Simply grant notification access, select the apps you want to monitor, and start building your notification history instantly.

**VIEW PREVIOUS NOTIFICATIONS**  
  
SummaryLog automatically records notifications as they arrive, making it easy to revisit messages, reminders, OTPs, delivery updates, shopping alerts, calendar notifications, and other important information.  
  
Instead of worrying about accidentally clearing notifications, simply open the app and browse your saved history whenever you need it.  
  
**CHAT-STYLE CONVERSATION VIEW**  
  
Notifications are organized into clean conversation threads, making them easier to read and navigate. This familiar layout helps you quickly review messages and follow conversations without scrolling through a traditional notification log and ask questions.

**AI-POWERED SUMMARY CHAT** ✅

Ask natural-language questions about your notification history and get instant, streaming answers. Powered by Google Gemini 1.5 Flash with a built-in keyword-match fallback engine that works completely offline when no API key is configured.

**SEARCH YOUR HISTORY**  
  
Need to find a specific notification? Use the built-in search feature to locate messages, alerts, reminders, and updates by keyword or content. Quickly find important information even within a large notification history and summarize it.
